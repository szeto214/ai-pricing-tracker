What is capacity-based pricing (Data Workers)?
Airbyte’s Pro plan is priced by Data Workers, a unit of capacity that powers your pipelines. There are multiple variables that we use to determine the cost of a Data Worker.
Each Data Worker can run multiple syncs concurrently; typically ~3 at once.
This model gives you predictable spend, guaranteed performance, and no charges based on data volume.
How is capacity-based pricing different from usage or volume-based?
Usage or volume-based pricing bills you by rows, GBs, or events. Costs grow as data grows and we know that not all data is equally valuable.
Airbyte uses capacity-based pricing, so you can control spend up-front, avoid surprise bills and can scale only when needed, so your data can grow without inflating your bill.
What is the Pro plan?
Pro includes everything in Standard, plus 15-minute syncs, custom mappings, premium support, and enhanced platform features like RBAC, row filtering, hashing & encryption, and support for multiple data regions. It’s built for production workloads that require reliability, control, and guaranteed performance.
What is Enterprise Flex?
Enterprise Flex is for enterprises that need sovereign data movement inside their own boundary, with deployment options including on-premises and multi-region, plus a hybrid model with an Airbyte managed control plane. It includes all Airbyte Pro features, including premium support.
What do you recommend for a small team of 3-4 developers?
We typically recommend starting with Standard (fully managed, volume-based) if you want zero infrastructure overhead and pay-as-you-go billing. Either way, you can always move to more robust pricing plans like Pro or Enterprise Flex as you scale and your needs change.
