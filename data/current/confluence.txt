About users & how you're billed for them
A
user
is someone who can log in to one of your Confluence sites and who exists in User Management. You can add and remove users as your team changes.
For
monthly
subscriptions, we'll charge you for the following month's subscription based on the exact number of Confluence users you have.
For
annual
subscriptions, we'll charge you for the tier that most closely matches your user count. Depending on the number of users purchased, annual subscriptions may be a better value.

or
