FAQ
What is JetBrains AI?
JetBrains AI is a service that provides access to AI models from leading providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, and xAI, as well as JetBrains' own model for code completion. It powers AI experiences across
JetBrains Air
, giving you access to coding agents like Codex, Claude Agent, Gemini CLI, and
Junie
by JetBrains.
How is JetBrains AI priced?
For individual users:
JetBrains AI is available in three AI credit plans: AI Free*, AI Pro, and AI Ultimate. Each plan includes a different number of AI credits, renewed every 30 days from the date you first activated your JetBrains AI license. Unused credits don't roll over. AI Pro and AI Ultimate users can purchase additional top-up credits if needed. Top-up credits remain valid for 12 months from the purchase date.
* AI Free isn't available in IntelliJ IDEA without Ultimate subscription and in PyCharm without Pro subscription, and Android Studio.
For teams and organizations:
Organizations don't have fixed JetBrains AI credit plans. They can purchase JetBrains AI credits in any amount for shared use across the organization. AI credits remain valid for 12 months from the purchase date.
Organizations can also choose an
AI governance plan
for centralized AI access, security, policies, analytics, and model management. Three plans are available: Basic (free), Business (free during early access), and Enterprise.
What are AI credits?
AI credits are the units used to pay for AI usage in JetBrains AI. One AI credit equals USD 1.00 of AI use (charged in your local currency). Usage of third-party AI models is billed at public API rates, with no surcharge.
Do you offer free AI access?
Individual users can use the
AI Free
plan, which includes three AI credits every 30 days. A free
30-day AI Pro trial
is also available when you first activate JetBrains AI in a JetBrains IDE.
In addition, AI credits are included at no additional cost with the
All Products Pack
and
dotUltimate
subscriptions*:
10 credits
every 30 days for individual users and
20 credits

*AI credits are not included in complimentary All Products Pack subscriptions for students, teachers, and classrooms, or for participants of the JetBrains Open-Source Program.
Can I use my own AI provider in JetBrains products?
Yes. You can connect supported AI providers using an existing provider account or your own API key. JetBrains AI is optional in these scenarios. Usage is billed directly by the provider and does not consume JetBrains AI credits.
