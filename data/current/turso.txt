Extra storage, reads, writes, and syncs beyond your plan are billed automatically once enabled, at per-unit rates that drop as you scale up.
How usage works
Plans include storage, rows read, rows written, and sync allowances, with predictable per-unit overage rates above the included amounts. Idle databases cost only storage, so a large fleet of mostly-idle databases stays cheap. Point-in-time restore and database branching do not add usage cost.
