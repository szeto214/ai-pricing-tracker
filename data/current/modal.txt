Why serverless?
Modal is serverless, which means that we instantly autoscale up and
down for you based on request volume. For spiky or unpredictable
workloads, we are more cost-effective than fixed on-demand/reserved
compute.
Traditional cloud
You’re billed for reserved GPUs, even when usage drops.
$5,400
75 GPUs * 24 hrs * $3 / GPU-hr
Degraded service at peak demand
Modal serverless cloud
Only pay for what you use, when you use it.
$4,740
Avg 50 GPUs * 24 hrs * $3.95 / GPU-hr
12% lower cost
