No more
surprise bills.
To protect your budget Rollbar gives you three ways to handle overages, so you always know what to expect when your event volume changes.
Stop at your plan limit.
On by default, if you prefer predictability, we’ll stop processing new events when you reach your plan’s monthly limit. No surprise charges, just a pause until your account renews.
On-demand events.
Need uninterrupted coverage? Enable on-demand and keep sending events beyond your plan limit, billed at the same rate as your standard plan. This ensures nothing is missed, but can lead to higher costs if volumes spike.
Overage budgets.
Set a dollar limit you’re comfortable exceeding above your base plan. Once your budget is reached, we pause processing, so you’re protected from unexpected costs while still covering short-term spikes.
