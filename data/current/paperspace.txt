Common Questions
FAQs
How does pricing work?
Your plan lets you use different Gradient tools and determines your limits. You are also charged for storage overages ($0.29/GB) and for compute usage (unless you are using a “free” instance types).
What forms of payment do you accept?
We use Stripe to process payments. We accept all major credit and debit cards including Visa, MasterCard, American Express and Discover. Custom invoicing is available on our business plans.
Contact our sales team
to learn more.
When will I be billed?
Billing occurs monthly and runs at midnight on the first of each month. You can check view your invoices on the billing tab and track compute utilization in realtime on the utilization tab.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
You can upgrade or cancel your paid subscriptions at anytime from within your console.
What's the difference between individual & team plans?
When you sign up for Gradient, you start with a "private workspace." This is your individual plan. To invite collaborators, create a new team and you'll see an option to invite other users. Your private workspace and team(s) are billed separately.
How do I cancel my account?
You can cancel your account anytime through the console, under the settings tab.
Can I set a billing alert or limit?
Yes! Navigate to the billing tab to control alerts and limits account-wide. On a team, you can also set individual alerts and limits on each user by opening the settings panel for a specific user.
Do you offer refunds?
No. Please open a support ticket if there are extenuating circumstances.
Is private data shared with advertisers?
No.

Common Questions
FAQs
How does pricing work?
For hourly plans you are charged a flat rate (depending on machine type) per month to cover storage and access. Additionally, you are charged an hourly rate when the machine is running. Monthly plans are offered at a flat rate for unlimited access.
Where are the servers located?
You can learn more about our global coverage
here
.
Am I charged when my machine is off?
For hourly plans, if your machine is powered off, you will only be billed for your storage. Monthly machines have a flat rate per month regardless if the machine is on or off.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
You can upgrade or cancel your paid subscriptions at anytime from within your console.
Do you offer refunds?
No. Please open a support ticket if there are extenuating circumstances.
How do I cancel my account?
You can cancel your account anytime through the console, under the settings tab.
Is private data shared with third parties or advertisers?
No.
