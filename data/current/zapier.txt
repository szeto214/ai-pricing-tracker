Frequently asked questions
Zapier overview
Do I need to know how to code?
Nope! Zapier makes it easy for you to move data between your apps without being a developer. We may need you to locate information for certain accounts, but we'll guide you through any additional steps. Our Support team is also here to help you via email should you need assistance.
What are apps?
An app is a web service or application, such as
Google Docs
,
Slack
, or
Salesforce
. Zapier offers integrations for more than 9,000 apps and helps you easily move data between them to automate repetitive tasks.
Premium apps
are select apps exclusively available to users on paid Zapier plans.
What is a Zap?
A Zap is an automated workflow that connects your apps and services together. Each Zap consists of a trigger and one or more actions. When you turn your Zap on, it will run the action steps every time the Zap is triggered.
What are tasks?
Tasks are used when a Zap successfully moves data or completes an action for you automatically. For example, if your Zap has an action to create new Google Contacts, each contact created will use one task. Some apps or actions may use more than one task when completed successfully. If the action isn't successfully completed, it doesn't count against your task limit.
What is a trigger?
A trigger is an event that starts a Zap. Once you turn on a Zap, Zapier will monitor for that trigger event. For example, if you create a Zap that sends a message to a Sales rep each time you get a new lead, the trigger is a new lead entering your lead management app. Triggers don't count toward your task limit.
What's the difference between Zap workflows, triggers, actions, and tasks?
Zap is our term for the automated workflows you set up to connect your apps. A trigger is the event that starts your Zap. Actions are events that happen automatically after your Zap is triggered. For example, in a Zap that sends a message to a Sales rep each time you get a new lead, the trigger is the new lead entering your lead management app, and the action is the Slack notification sent with the new lead's info. When Zapier performs an action successfully, it counts as a task. Each successful action in a Zap counts as a separate task.
Does checking (or polling) for new data use tasks?
Zapier never charges you a task to check for new data—only when a Zap successfully completes an action for you. For instance, say you want an automated workflow to check—or poll—your Dropbox account every five minutes for new files. Then, if a new file appears, the workflow will send you a Slack message. In this case, sending the Slack message about the new Dropbox file counts as a task.
How many tasks do I need?
The number of tasks you need depends on the number of active Zap workflows you have and how frequently those Zap workflows run to automate your work. The more actions your Zap workflows complete, the more tasks you'll need.
But don't worry! When you reach your plan's task limit, we'll notify you via email. When you reach your plan's task limit, we'll also switch you to
pay-per-task billing
.
What happens if I hit my task limit?
We'll email you when you're close to reaching your plan's task limit, and then again when you reach it.
If you turn on pay-per-task billing, we'll switch you to pay-per-task billing once you reach your plan's task limit so your Zap workflows continue running. Pay-per-task billing is charged at a higher per-task rate than your base subscription tasks.
When you're on pay-per-task billing, we'll email you if you're close to your plan's maximum task limit and then again when you reach it. Once you've reached your maximum task limit, your Zap workflows will pause until the start of your next usage period.
If you have pay-per-task billing turned off, your Zap workflows will pause once you reach your plan's task limit until the start of your next usage period.

Tables
How can I try Tables?
Tables is now included as part of your Zapier plan. You can create an unlimited number of Tables. The number of records you can have across these Tables will vary depending on which plan tier you are on.
How is Tables different from other options out there?
Tables connects seamlessly with other Zapier tools and features like Zap workflows and Forms. That means you can confidently build data systems with automation naturally integrated and easily connect to Zapier's 9,000+ apps.
Forms
How can I try Forms?
Forms is now included as part of your Zapier plan. You can create an unlimited number of Forms. The number of pages you can have across these Forms will vary depending on which plan tier you are on.
How is Forms different from other options out there?
Zapier Forms is the only product on the market that connects your form, web page, or app with more than 9,000 integrations—without any coding required. When you combine Forms with Zapier's powerful automation tools, you can build flexible, tailored solutions in minutes.
MCP
What is Zapier MCP?
Zapier Model Context Protocol allows your AI apps to securely interact with tools. Zapier MCP connects you to Zapier's vast ecosystem of 9,000 integrations.
How much does Zapier MCP cost?
Zapier MCP is available to all accounts. One MCP tool call uses two tasks from your Zapier plan's quota.
Is Zapier MCP secure?
Yes, MCP endpoints include built-in authentication for secure, reliable connections.

SDK
What is Zapier SDK?
Zapier SDK lets anyone securely interact with Zapier's ecosystem of 9,000+ apps from their own code, agents, or command-line tools. You can use it to discover apps, inspect available actions and inputs, manage connections, and run actions across Zapier's integrations.
How much does Zapier SDK cost?
Zapier SDK is free while in beta. We'll notify you before the beta ends, and before any changes to pricing or task usage take effect.

Should I use the Team or Enterprise plan?
The Team plan provides features that facilitate collaboration on automation across several groups or teams. These features include shared app connections and a shared workspace. The Enterprise plan enables your entire team to use Zapier, with advanced security features, governance, and monitoring features such as advanced admin permissions, single sign-on, and custom data retention. Enterprise allows you to empower all of your employees while providing safeguards you need to protect sensitive data and critical workflows.
Which plans allow for unlimited users?
Unlimited users are available on Team and Enterprise plans.

Agents FAQs
What is an activity?
Activities include actions your agent takes in behaviors or in chat, browsing the web, or looking up information from attached knowledge.
How are activities counted for Zapier Team accounts?
If you have a Team account on Zapier, your team shares a pool of Agents activities.
Can I use Agents with my Zapier Enterprise account?
Zapier Agents doesn't currently support app and action restrictions that may be in place on an Enterprise account.
How do I improve the reliability of agents?
You can improve your agent's reliability by following a few of our tips for writing an effective AI prompt.
Why don't I get the same outcome from AI every time?
That's because of non-determinism—what makes LLMs different from computers, which will do the same thing every single time.
What app actions can agents perform on my behalf?
Zapier Agents can only take actions in the apps you've connected and use the triggers and actions you've set up.
