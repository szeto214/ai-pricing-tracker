Frequently asked questions

What is chat to automation?
It's the quickest way to get work off your plate. Chat with your tools to get answers and take action, then turn the boring, repetitive parts into automations as fast as you can describe them. We're rolling it out gradually, so it might not be on your account yet.
Is the free plan really free?
Yes. No card, no time limit. It's meant for exploring the product and building lightweight automations. You get 100 credits a day, which is 100 flow runs, and a fresh batch lands every morning — so if you use them all, you wait until tomorrow.
How does pricing work?
Mostly credits: you pay for what your automations actually run. Beyond that, the plan you're on sets how many people can use it and which features you get.
What is a credit?
A credit is the single unit behind everything your automations do. Running a flow costs 1 credit, however many steps are in it. AI steps cost more, 2 to 20 depending on the model, or 1 if you bring your own AI key.
How many credits do I get?
Free gives you 100 credits a day, refreshed every morning. Plus includes 10,000 credits a month and Team includes 50,000, both resetting monthly, and you can keep running past them at $0.007 per extra credit. Ultimate is sized to how you actually run and pooled across the year, so a heavy month is covered by a quiet one.
What happens when I run out of credits?
Depends on your plan. On Free, your flows stay switched on but any runs past your 100 a day won't go through until credits refresh the next morning. On Plus and Team they keep running at $0.007 per extra credit. Ultimate has a yearly pool, so one heavy month is fine.
Can I self-host for free?
Yes. Community Edition is open source, free forever, with no cap on runs, users, or flows. It's the automation core on its own, so a fair amount sits outside it: Agents and Chat, Projects, API access, and the whole team and admin layer, meaning SSO, user roles, audit logs, secret managers, branding, and Git Sync. Support is the community, not our team.
Why would I pay if I self-host?
Self-hosting decides where Activepieces runs, not what it can do. Paying adds the layer you need once more than a couple of people depend on it: controlling who has access, seeing who changed what, keeping teams and clients separate, shipping changes safely, and running Agents and Chat. It also puts our team on the other end instead of a forum.
What if my tool isn't supported?
You're not stuck. Anything with an API works right away through the HTTP piece or a webhook. For a proper integration, pieces are open source TypeScript, so you or we can build one.
What enterprise features do you have?
SSO, standard roles, and email support start on Team. Ultimate adds SCIM provisioning, custom roles, audit logs, secret managers, event streaming, private pieces, branding, Git Sync with releases, and a dedicated support contact. All of it runs on our cloud or on your own servers.
