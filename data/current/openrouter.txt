Meta: Muse Image
Muse Image
18+
318M tokens
Muse Image is an agentic image generation model from Meta that generates and edits images from text and reference images. Unlike single-pass image models, it reasons before it renders, breaking down multi-part prompts and refining its output within the chain of thought, and invokes web search for factual accuracy on knowledge-intensive prompts. The model supports text-to-image generation, targeted image editing, multi-image composition, reference-image conditioning for style and subject consistency across a series, and precise text rendering within generated images. Iterative editing works by passing the previous output image back with a new instruction.
